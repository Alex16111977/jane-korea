🌸 About

Miso: 한국어 수업 is an interactive platform for learning Korean, created with a passion for Korean culture. Our goal is to make learning Korean accessible, engaging and effective.

The platform follows the Korean proficiency level system (1급-6급), which corresponds to the international TOPIK standards. You start with simple greetings and gradually progress to complex texts and grammar structures.
