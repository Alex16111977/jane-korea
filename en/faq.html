💡 Tips

We recommend alternating between activities: start with a grammar lesson, then read a text on the topic, then practice words in the vocabulary section. Study regularly for at least 15-20 minutes a day — this is more effective than long, infrequent sessions.
On average, each level takes 2-4 months with daily practice. Beginner levels (1-2급) are usually completed faster, while advanced levels (5-6급) require more time and practice. Everyone learns at their own pace — the key is consistency.
